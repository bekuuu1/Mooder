## Brand & Style

This design system defines an ultra-premium, precision-engineered dark-mode environment for deep audio telemetry and music intelligence. Borrowing the functional density and refined restraint of high-end developer tooling alongside vibrant, luminescent sonic visualizers, the interface channels the feel of an advanced audio workstation fused with enterprise analytics.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Surgical Precision:** Every metric, curve, and waveform conveys technical authority and sub-millisecond responsiveness.
- **Electric Lucidity:** Vibrant spectrum accents punctuate an obsidian void, turning complex multi-dimensional audio data (valence, energy, acousticness) into immediate spatial insights.
- **Understated Luxury:** Surfaces avoid heavy opacities or aggressive gradients, relying instead on frosted obsidian panes, razor-thin luminous borders, and subtle radial light leaks.

### Design Movement
**Futuristic Dark Glass & Data-Dense Minimalism.** The style combines deep slate/obsidian foundations with ultra-fine structural dividers (1px borders at low opacity), hairline frosted backdrops, and localized emissive glows. Monospaced tabular data anchors the analytics, while radiant neon vectors illuminate interactive radar charts and multi-axis scatterplots.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an infinite dark void layered with tonal slate tiers, illuminated by high-chroma spectral accents tied to audio dimensions.

### Surface Architecture
- **Void Surface (`#090D16`):** The primary canvas background; an uncompromising, deep obsidian.
- **Card & Panel Layer (`#0D111C`):** Base container elevation with minimal reflectance.
- **Interactive Elevated Surface (`#131A29`):** Hover states, popovers, drawers, and nested modules.
- **Subtle Surface Stroke (`#1F293D`):** Primary structural boundary for cards and data tables.
- **High-Contrast Stroke (`#2E3B52`):** Focus rings, active tabs, and highlighted container borders.

### Sonic Dimension Spectrum
- **Spotify Emerald (`#1DB954`):** Primary brand actions, streaming telemetry, health states, and baseline metrics.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00F2FE`):** High-frequency metrics, danceability indicators, audio transients, and primary timeline markers.
- **Neon Violet (`#8A2BE2`):** Acousticness, instrumentalness, complex harmonics, and secondary clusters.
- **Sonic Coral (`#FF4B72`):** High-energy thresholds, tempo peaks, loudness extremes, and critical variance.
- **Warm Amber (`#FFB300`):** Valence axis, emotional warmth, key signature highlights, and warnings.

### Text & Iconography Tiers
- **Text High-Contrast (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary headlines, active metric values, and primary labels.
- **Text Mid-Contrast (`#94A3B8`):** Body copy, secondary labels, disabled inputs, and column headers.
- **Text Low-Contrast (`#475569`):** Minor axis labels, grid units, inactive track details, and watermarks.

## Typography

The typography couples the refined neo-grotesque personality of Geist with the mechanical clarity of JetBrains Mono. 

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Rigor:** All scientific measurements (BPM, LUFS, Key, Valence scale `0.00 - 1.00`, Danceability ratios) must strictly utilize `JetBrains Mono` with tabular numbers enabled (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during live audio processing.
- **Display Weights:** Headlines never exceed a weight of `600`. The visual distinction relies on negative letter tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) rather than massive strokes.
- **Labels & Tags:** Audio tags, track keys, Camelot wheel markers, and timecode readouts utilize `label-code` in all caps with expanded letter spacing (`0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system, designed for high-density audio monitoring and dashboard modularity.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`gutter-lg`) gutters and 32px (`margin-lg`) margin padding. Supports docked visualizer docks, audio inspectors, and dual scatterplot grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`gutter`) gutters and 24px (`margin-md`) margins. Telemetry KPI cards collapse into 2x2 matrices; charts default to tabbed views rather than side-by-side comparison.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins and 12px horizontal component gaps. Visual charts expand to full container width with horizontal card scrolling for audio feature breakdowns.

### Spacing Principles
- Density takes precedence over expansive white space. Internal component paddings rely primarily on `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (12px) to maximize the visible data canvas.
- Spacers between data visualization groups maintain a strict `space-xl` (32px) separation to retain structural hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via subtle luminance steps, ultra-crisp hairline borders, and localized emissive radial blooms instead of drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Surface 0 (Deep Canvas):** `#090D16` solid background. Zero shadow.
2. **Surface 1 (Base Panels & Charts):** `#0D111C` with a 1px border of `#1F293D`. Frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with 85% opacity) when floating over waveform canvases.
3. **Surface 2 (Floating Inspect Modals & Tooltips):** `#131A29` with a 1px border of `#2E3B52` and an ambient diffuse glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
4. **Surface 3 (Active Sonic Focus):** Applied to active playback states or selected nodes. Layered with an inner rim-light: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and an electric primary accent bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(29, 185, 84, 0.25)`.

### Border & Light Rules
- All borders must be exactly 1px thick. Never use blurred borders.
- Gradient border strokes are reserved exclusively for active audio metrics undergoing real-time modulation or focus.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, architectural contour profile (`roundedness: 1`). Soft, controlled micro-radii reinforce technical discipline and instrument design.

### Corner Radius System
- **Micro Elements (Pills, Tags, Code Chips):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains razor sharpness for small inline data elements.
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Sliders):** `6px` (`0.375rem`). Balanced ergonomic touchpoints that sit cleanly within dense grids.
- **Panels & KPI Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Structural frame radius.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`). Outer envelope curves for floating sheets.
- **Circular Elements:** Strictly reserved for playback toggles (Play/Pause), node markers on scatterplots, and Camelot wheel visualizers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sonic Action:** Solid Spotify Emerald (`#1DB954`) background, text in black (`#090D16`), font weight `500`. Hover brings a 10% brightness increase and an electric aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(29, 185, 84, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#1F293D`, text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover switches border to `#2E3B52` and surface to `#131A29`.
- **Icon Buttons (Transport / Audio Scrubber):** 36x36px square with 6px border-radius, background `#0D111C`, border 1px solid `#1F293D`, icon stroke `#94A3B8`. Hover transitions stroke to `#00F2FE`.

### KPI & Metric Cards
- **Structure:** Obsidian glass panel (`#0D111C` at 90% opacity), 1px border `#1F293D`, internal padding `space-md`.
- **Header:** Metric label in `label-code` uppercase `#94A3B8` paired with an accent dot (e.g., Electric Cyan for Danceability).
- **Value Display:** High-contrast `metric-display` in JetBrains Mono (`#F8FAFC`), trailing unit in `metric-sm` (`#475569`).
- **Footer:** Micro sparkline or range delta bar displaying percentiles against the playlist or global corpus average.

### Audio Feature Sliders
- **Track:** 4px height, background `#1F293D`, rounded-full.
- **Active Range Fill:** Gradient matching the selected sonic parameter (e.g., Valence: `#FFB300` to `#FF4B72`).
- **Thumb:** 14px circle, solid `#F8FAFC` core with a 1px border in `#090D16`, glowing with the active gradient's dominant color. Focus state expands an emissive ring of 4px at 20% opacity.

### Chips & Feature Filters
- **Default State:** Background `#0D111C`, border 1px solid `#1F293D`, text `metric-sm` `#94A3B8`.
- **Selected State:** Border color inherits the respective feature color (e.g., `#00F2FE`), text `#F8FAFC`, background tinted with 10% feature hex.

### Data Tables & Track Lists
- **Row Architecture:** Height 44px, alternating transparent and `#0D111C` rows. 
- **Borders:** Bottom-only 1px hairline divider in `#131A29`.
- **Columns:** Track metadata in `Geist`, numerical attributes (BPM, Key, Loudness) right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Active / Playing Row:** Subtle left border in `#1DB954` (2px solid), text shifted to `#1DB954`, and background elevated to `#131A29`.

### Scatterplot & Radar Nodes
- **Data Points:** 6px SVG circles with 1.5px stroke `#090D16`. Fill driven by the Valence/Energy quadrant mapping:
  - High Energy / High Valence: `#FFB300`
  - High Energy / Low Valence: `#FF4B72`
  - Low Energy / Low Valence: `#8A2BE2`
  - Low Energy / High Valence: `#00F2FE`
- **Hover Crosshair:** 1px dashed `#2E3B52` guideline with sticky HUD tooltip indicating song title and exact vector values.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Input:** Background `#090D16`, border 1px solid `#1F293D`, color `#F8FAFC`, font family `Geist`, padding `8px 12px`.
- **Focus:** Border transitions to `#00F2FE`, no default browser outline, faint cyan glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #00F2FE, 0 0 8px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.2)`).
